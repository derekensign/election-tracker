    Sources & method

      Ratings tables, poll tables, and legislative district tables are read from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), which cites each forecaster and pollster. Polls also come from VoteHub's public feed. Odds come from the Polymarket and Kalshi public APIs; a market is marked thin below $10k traded. The seat distribution labelled "derived" is a Poisson-binomial over per-race win probabilities (Polymarket, then Kalshi, then a rating-based prior) that assumes races are independent, so it understates the chance of a uniform swing. District boundaries: Texas Legislative Council plans H2316 and S2168. Not affiliated with any campaign, forecaster, or exchange.
